The Transaction Enrichment Advantage

Why traditional banks and legacy aggregators (Plaid, TrueLayer, Tink) fall short—and why our 3-tier cascade represents a quantum leap in financial data quality.

1

Why Banks Withhold Categorization

Open Banking regulations (PSD2/CMA Order) only mandate raw ledger entries (dates, amounts, and raw narratives). Banks deliberately hoard categorization models to protect their retail apps, leaving raw data messy and unstandardized.

2

The Aggregator Mark-Up Trap

Aggregators sell basic enrichment as an expensive enterprise add-on (5p to 10p+ per transaction). For an enterprise syncing millions of transactions, third-party enrichment fees become a massive recurring expense.

3

Semantic Contextual Intelligence

Legacy enrichers use dumb regexes that confuse £2.99 iCloud software with a £1,499 MacBook, or trip over POS prefixes like SUMUP *. Our engine reasons about amount, timing, payment rails, and HMRC tax deductibility.

B2B Integration Quickstart

Any authorized application can consume the enrichment pipeline directly via standard JSON REST endpoints: 

# Enrich a single transaction
curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/v1/enrich/transaction \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"raw_narrative": "Welcome Brentford", "amount": -31.46}'

# Batch enrichment (up to 100 transactions in parallel)
curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/v1/enrich/batch \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"transactions": [
    {"raw_narrative": "SWITCH2 ENERGY LTD", "amount": -42.35},
    {"raw_narrative": "APPLE.COM/BILL", "amount": -2.99}
  ]}'
